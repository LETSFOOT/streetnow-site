01 / THE PROBLEM

The hardest part shouldn’t be getting a game together.

You want to play. But finding a pitch, a match and enough people can mean endless messages and plans that never happen.

OUR SOLUTION

One place to turn “who’s playing?” into a plan.

StreetNow brings local football into a mobile app: discover places to play, organize a match and connect with people who share your love of the game.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

From your neighbourhood
to your next match.

Core features are implemented in the current development build. Public availability will follow launch; planned additions are marked below.

MADE IN FRANCE

03 / ABOUT STREETNOW

Built in Bordeaux.
Made for the local game.

StreetNow, formerly LetsFoot, is an independent mobile project developed in France. The ambition is simple: make it easier for people to play football together, starting locally and growing towards major French cities.

A project shaped by everyday football — the pitch around the corner, a free evening and people ready to play.
